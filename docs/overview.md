# Overview — Fynd

## Vision

To become the definitive digital home for youth sport in Slovakia — and eventually across Central Europe — where every player, coach, parent and fan has everything they need in one place.

## Mission

Fynd eliminates the friction between sports clubs and their communities. We replace scattered WhatsApp groups, random Googling for results, manual paperwork and zero digital motivation with a single, engaging platform that makes showing up feel rewarding.

## The Name

**Fynd** = *find* + *fandom*

- **Find** — the core utility: find your team's results, your club, your stats, your community
- **Fandom** — the emotional layer: the passion, the loyalty, the identity that surrounds every sport

The name was chosen to be sport-agnostic from day one. Fynd works for football today, but is designed to extend to hockey, basketball, handball, athletics — any team sport with clubs, players and fans.

**Tagline:** *Beyond the Score*

The tagline signals that Fynd is more than a results app. It is about the community, the journey, the streaks, the friendships — everything that happens beyond the scoreline.

---

## The Gap We Are Filling

Slovakia has:
- **2,800+** registered football clubs
- **180,000+** registered SFZ players
- **0** dedicated community platforms for youth clubs

What exists today:
- Sportnet.online — results database, no community layer
- Facebook groups — unstructured, no permissions, no notifications
- WhatsApp groups — chaotic, no calendar, no history
- No streak or reward system anywhere

Fynd sits in the empty space between "broadcast results tool" and "social media" — a purpose-built community layer for organised sport.

## Primary Users

### 1. Youth Players (8–18 years)
**Motivation:** They want recognition, progression and fun. They respond to streaks, XP, rankings and digital rewards — the same mechanics that make Duolingo and Fortnite sticky.

**Pain points today:**
- No digital acknowledgment for showing up to training
- No visibility of personal progress
- No connection to other players outside their own club

**What Fynd gives them:**
- Training streak with visual rewards
- XP points redeemable for real club rewards
- Personal profile with avatar, stats and badges
- Leaderboards at club, regional and national level

### 2. Coaches
**Motivation:** They want to spend time coaching, not doing admin.

**Pain points today:**
- Attendance tracking is manual or done via chat polls
- Training schedule changes require messaging multiple groups
- No structured post-match feedback channel
- Transfer paperwork duplicated between Sportnet and club records

**What Fynd gives them:**
- One-click attendance marking
- Announcements broadcast to the right category instantly
- Post-match ratings (anonymous, results visible to management)
- Sportnet sync eliminates transfer duplication

### 3. Parents
**Motivation:** They want to stay informed and involved without being overwhelmed.

**Pain points today:**
- Finding match results requires checking 3–4 different places
- Training cancellations arrive late or not at all
- No visibility into their child's progress and engagement

**What Fynd gives them:**
- Live match ticker with per-club notification settings
- Instant push for training changes
- Progress dashboard for their child
- Included in U9–U13 chats automatically

### 4. Club Management
**Motivation:** Retain members, reduce admin load, build club identity.

**Pain points today:**
- No data on which players are engaged or at risk of dropping out
- Reward programmes are ad hoc and hard to manage
- No digital club identity beyond a Facebook page

**What Fynd gives them:**
- Engagement data (attendance streaks, XP activity)
- Configurable reward shop — club sets the prizes
- Sportnet integration for transfers and results
- Seasonal themes reinforce club identity digitally

---

## Sport-Agnostic by Design

Fynd launched with Slovak football as the first use case — it has the highest number of clubs and players, and the clearest pain around Sportnet integration.

However, every feature was designed without football-specific dependencies:
- "Training" works for any sport
- "Match" works for any game format
- "Category" (U9, U15, A-team) maps to any age-group sport
- Sportnet integration is modular — other federation APIs can be added

The roadmap includes hockey, basketball and handball integrations in F5 onward.
