# Branding — Fynd

## Name

**Fynd**

Pronunciation: /faɪnd/ (like "find") — or colloquially "fajnd" in Slovak  
Origin: *find* + *fandom*

The name is intentionally short, memorable, and sport-agnostic. It works in Slovak, English, and across Central Europe.

---

## Tagline

**Beyond the Score**

Signals that Fynd is about more than results — it's about community, progress, identity and the journey behind every scoreline.

## Mascot — Futbot

Futbot is Fynd's robot mascot. A friendly, tech-forward character designed to be recognisable to both young players (gaming aesthetic) and parents (non-threatening, approachable).

### Design
- **Head:** Rectangular with rounded corners, dark navy body
- **Visor:** Wide horizontal visor with glowing green eyes (two rectangular eye panels)
- **Antenna:** Single antenna with blinking green tip
- **Body:** Clean rectangular torso with FN badge, LED status strip
- **Arms:** Segmented, one raised in greeting, one at side
- **Legs:** Short, stubby with teal boot trim
- **Colour palette:** Dark navy (#06101E), teal green (#00D97E), white highlights

### Animations
- Antenna tip blinks every ~1.8 seconds
- Whole body bobs up and down gently (2.8s cycle)
- Eyes pulse alternately
- Scan line moves across visor

### Usage
Futbot appears on:
- Splash/onboarding screen
- Empty states (no results yet, no chat messages)
- Achievement celebrations
- Error screens
- Marketing materials

### What Futbot is NOT
- Not aggressive or competitive-looking
- Not football-specific (works for all sports)
- Not childish to the point of alienating older teens

## Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Headings | Space Grotesk | 700 | 32 – 88px |
| Body | Nunito | 400 – 800 | 10 – 20px |
| Monospace / Stats | Space Grotesk | 700 | varies |

Space Grotesk is used for high-impact display text — scores, levels, headings. Nunito is used for all body copy, buttons and UI text. The combination feels modern, sporty and approachable.

## Voice & Tone

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Direct and confident | Formal or corporate |
| Warm and encouraging | Condescending |
| Sport-literate without jargon | Overly technical |
| Slovak-first, globally readable | Anglicisms that don't land in Slovak |
| Celebrate small wins | Ignore effort |

**Example (streak reminder):**  
✅ "Dnes ešte netrénuješ? Tvoj streak na teba čaká 🔥"  
❌ "Upozornenie: Vaša séria tréningov je ohrozená."
