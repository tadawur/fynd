# Target Audience — Fynd

## Persona 1: Lukáš, 14 — The Young Player

**Location:** Nová Baňa  
**Club:** MFK Nová Baňa, U15  
**Devices:** iPhone, Xbox

**Behaviour:**
- Plays Fortnite and FIFA daily
- Has Duolingo streak of 42 days (English)
- Checks Instagram 6× per day
- WhatsApp group for his team has 34 members and 200+ messages per week

**Frustrations:**
- Nobody acknowledges when he scores or has a good training
- His stats don't exist anywhere
- He can't see how he compares to players at other clubs
- Getting information about training schedule means scrolling through 200 messages

**What Fynd gives Lukáš:**
- Streak counter that validates his consistency
- XP for every goal and training — visible on his profile
- Leaderboard position (he's #3 in his club right now, wants to be #1)
- Clean profile he can link from his Instagram bio
- Notifications that tell him exactly what changed, when

**Key metric:** Opens app 3–4× per week, maintains streak for 28+ days

## Persona 2: Jana, 38 — The Parent

**Location:** Žiar nad Hronom  
**Child:** Tomáš, 11, FK Žiar U11  
**Devices:** Android, laptop

**Behaviour:**
- Works full-time, picks Tomáš up from training twice a week
- In 4 WhatsApp groups related to Tomáš's football
- Occasionally misses training cancellations because of notification overload

**Frustrations:**
- Last week she drove to training that was cancelled an hour earlier
- She had to ask three people what the score was at Saturday's match
- She doesn't know how engaged Tomáš is or how he compares to peers
- No overview of the upcoming schedule — it's scattered across group chats

**What Fynd gives Jana:**
- Training cancellation notification that she cannot miss (always-on)
- Live score push during matches — she follows the game from work
- Simple dashboard showing Tomáš's attendance and streak
- One calendar view for the next 6 weeks of fixtures and training

**Key metric:** Opens app 1–2× per week, zero missed cancellations

## Persona 3: Milan, 42 — The Coach

**Location:** Kremnica  
**Club:** TJ Kremnica, coaches U13 and U15  
**Devices:** Android

**Behaviour:**
- Volunteers as coach 3 evenings + Saturday per week
- Manages two WhatsApp groups (separate for each category)
- Writes the same information in both groups plus to parents separately
- Does attendance in a paper notebook

**Frustrations:**
- Spends 20 minutes after every training sending the same messages to different groups
- Transfers require him to update Sportnet, the club spreadsheet and the WhatsApp groups separately
- Has no way to see which players are at risk of dropping out
- Parents message him directly on personal WhatsApp at all hours

**What Fynd gives Milan:**
- One announcement → correct groups receive it automatically
- Attendance marking takes 30 seconds with a tap-through player list
- Sportnet sync means transfer updates happen automatically
- Post-match ratings give him structured (anonymous) player feedback
- Engagement dashboard shows him who hasn't opened the app in 2 weeks

**Key metric:** Marks attendance after 90%+ of sessions, sends announcements via Fynd instead of WhatsApp

## Persona 4: Petra, 52 — The Club Secretary

**Location:** Nová Baňa  
**Club:** MFK Nová Baňa, secretary + treasurer  
**Devices:** Windows laptop, basic Android

**Behaviour:**
- Manages all administrative communication for the club
- Currently uses Excel for member tracking, paper for transfers
- Sends club-wide emails for AGMs and events
- Not very technical but capable of learning straightforward interfaces

**Frustrations:**
- No overview of which players are actually active vs just registered
- Reward programme (kit, equipment) is tracked in a spreadsheet and prone to errors
- No way to identify which players are most engaged for awards at the AGM
- Transfer requests require back-and-forth with Sportnet manually

**What Fynd gives Petra:**
- Member overview with engagement stats (last login, streak, XP)
- Digital reward shop — no spreadsheet required
- Automatic Sportnet sync for transfers
- End-of-season award suggestions based on data

**Key metric:** Logs in weekly, processes at least 1 reward order per month

## Secondary Audience

### Scouts / Talent Agents
Not a primary user but a potential future audience. National leaderboard filtered by age, position and region gives scouts a structured view of engaged, consistent players.

Privacy note: this feature requires explicit opt-in from players and parents.

### Sports Journalists
Regional media cover local club results. Fynd's public match feed provides a clean, embeddable data source for match reports. Partnership opportunity in Phase 5.
