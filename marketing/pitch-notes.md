# Pitch Notes — Fynd

## The One-Liner

> Fynd is the first community platform for Slovak youth sports clubs — live results, training streaks, XP rewards and club communication, all in one place.

---

## The Hook (for parents)

*"When did your child's club last play? What was the score? Which players scored?"*

Most parents in Slovak football cannot answer these questions without checking Sportnet, a Facebook group, and asking in a WhatsApp chat. Fynd puts all of it in one notification.

---

## The Hook (for clubs)

*"How many players have you lost to other activities in the last three years?"*

Slovak clubs lose members every year not because of bad coaching — but because sport cannot compete digitally with games, social media and YouTube. Fynd gives clubs the digital tools to stay relevant.

---

## The Hook (for players 10–16)

*"You've been playing for two years. Can you prove it?"*

Without Fynd, a young player's entire sporting career is invisible — no stats, no badges, no recognition beyond a trophy photo on Facebook. Fynd makes every session, every goal and every streak visible and permanent.

## Why Now

1. **Sportnet adoption is high** — the infrastructure for official data exists, Fynd just connects to it
2. **Post-COVID digital shift** — clubs that survived COVID are actively looking for digital tools
3. **Generation Z is the player base** — the cohort that responds to streaks, XP and digital recognition is exactly the target demographic
4. **Zero direct competition** — no Slovak or Czech product addresses this specific combination of features
